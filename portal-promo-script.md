# Bur Oaks Camper Portal Promo Video Script

Length: about 72 seconds  
Use: open `/camper-portal-promo.html`, tap **Start promo with sound**, then record it full-screen for Facebook, the website, or a launch text/email.

The page includes browser-generated narration and a soft campfire ambience. Browsers block automatic sound, so the start button is required.

## Scene 1 — Opening

On screen: “Camp life just got a serious upgrade.”

Voiceover: “Bur Oaks campers, the new camper portal is here — and camp life just got a serious upgrade.”

## Scene 2 — Portal Reveal

On screen: “Everything for your stay. One easy place.”

Voiceover: “Invoices, AutoPay, ACH bank payments, weather, documents, electric history, maintenance requests, dinner signups, the camper directory, office messages, and important alerts — all from your phone.”

## Scene 3 — Real Bur Oaks Tools

On screen: “Less guessing. More camping.”

Voiceover: “See clear itemized invoices, set up AutoPay, use low-fee ACH instead of writing checks, view assigned documents, and track current and past electric kilowatt usage.”

## Scene 4 — Events

On screen: “Events finally feel easy to follow.”

Voiceover: “Tap event flyers, see the details, RSVP, and stay connected to what’s happening next at Bur Oaks.”

## Scene 5 — Saturday Dinners

On screen: “Saturday night at 6.”

Voiceover: “For Saturday night dinners, you can let us know who’s coming, how many guests you’re bringing, and what you’d like to bring.”

## Scene 6 — Alerts

On screen: “Important notices won’t get buried.”

Voiceover: “Storm alerts, bill reminders, office messages, dinner updates, maintenance notices, electric updates, and urgent campground updates can all be sent through the portal.”

## Scene 7 — Built for Bur Oaks

On screen: “Not some generic app. Bur Oaks, our way.”

Voiceover: “This was built around the way Bur Oaks really works — seasonal sites, AutoPay, ACH, electric billing, kilowatt history, pump-outs, leases, events, meals, maintenance, directory opt-in, and real campground life.”

## Scene 8 — Call to Action

On screen: “Your Bur Oaks camper portal is ready.”

Voiceover: “Watch for your invite, set your password, update your profile, turn on alerts, set up AutoPay if you want it, and start using the portal built just for Bur Oaks campers. Less paperwork. Less guessing. More camping.”
